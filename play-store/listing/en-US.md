**Full description (1434/4000):**

Enter GHOSTNET, a fictional cyber-themed idle clicker where every tap strengthens your network.

Start with a basic laptop, earn Bitcoin inside the game, and steadily grow a network of automated hacking equipment. Choose simulated targets, collect rewards, and unlock stronger upgrades as your operation expands.

BUILD YOUR NETWORK
• Tap to complete simulated breaches and earn in-game BTC.
• Upgrade 16 different hardware and software items.
• Recruit hosts and improve automated production.
• Experiment with target tiers and unique reward mechanics.

ASCEND AND PRESTIGE
• Level items toward 9,999 and unlock item ascension abilities.
• Execute ZERO DAY resets to earn permanent research bonuses.
• Complete classified operations, contracts, and achievements.

COME BACK FOR MORE
• Claim daily login bonuses and complete daily objectives.
• Discover rare events, milestone rewards, and temporary boosts.
• Keep playing as a guest or choose optional Google Play Games cloud saves when available.

FAIR FREE-TO-PLAY DESIGN
GHOSTNET is free to play. Optional rewarded ads and Premium Credit offers are planned; they do not lock core progression. Availability of ads and purchases depends on the release version.

GHOSTNET is a single-player fiction/simulation game. It does not perform real network intrusions or mine real cryptocurrency. BTC, Premium Credits, and ZERO DAY tokens are in-game resources without real-world cash value.
