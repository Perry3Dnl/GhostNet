## Full description
Enter GhostNet, a cyber-themed idle hacking simulation.

Start with basic hardware and build your own digital hacking empire. Breach virtual systems, collect simulated Bitcoin and upgrade your infrastructure to increase your hacking power.

Build your network:
- Tap to hack virtual systems.
- Compromise hosts and expand your network.
- Collect simulated Bitcoin.
- Upgrade your hardware.
- Increase your hacking power and passive income.
- Progress through the prestige system.
- Activate boosts to accelerate your progress.

GhostNet combines incremental progression with a clean, dark terminal-inspired interface.

How powerful can your network become?

GhostNet is a fictional single-player simulation. It does not perform real hacking, mine real cryptocurrency or provide real-world financial rewards.
